Toronto Move privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Applies to the Toronto Move apps for iPhone and Android and the Toronto Move website.

In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold. Your location and the places you search for are used only to plan trips and find departures. If you turn on notifications, the server keeps your notification settings and the trips you watch until you turn notifications off.

What the app uses, and why

InformationWhat happens to it
Your location (only if you allow it)Used to plan trips from where you are, show nearby departures and Bike Share stations, and rank search results. It is sent to our server with that one request and not stored. You can say no and type an address instead.
Places you search for and trips you planSent to our server to look up the address and the routes. Not stored on the server.
Home, Work and recent placesSaved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
Notification token and settings (only if you turn notifications on)A random token from Apple, Google or your browser that lets us send you alerts, plus your choices (weather warning level, routes you follow). Stored on our server so it can send alerts.
Watched trips (only if you use “Watch this trip”)The start and end points, time and days of the trip, so the server can check it for delays and remind you. These points may be your home or work, so only watch trips you are comfortable storing. Removing a watch, or turning notifications off, deletes it.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or payment details, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

Services that receive part of a request

To answer your request, our server passes only what is needed to these services. They receive it from our server, not from your device, except the map and push services listed last.

  • Transitous (open-source trip planner): start and end points and time of a trip.
  • Photon by komoot (address search, OpenStreetMap data): the text you search for and an approximate location to rank nearby results.
  • TTC, Metrolinx (GO Transit, UP Express), Bike Share Toronto, Environment and Climate Change Canada, AeroDataBox: our server downloads live times, alerts, weather and flights from them. No information about you is sent.
  • Maps: Apple Maps (iPhone), Google Maps (Android) or OpenStreetMap tiles (website) load the map area you are looking at directly on your device, under their own privacy policies.
  • Notifications: alerts are delivered through Expo’s push service and Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging (phones), or your browser’s push service (website). They receive your notification token and the alert text.

Our server is hosted by Render. Like most web hosts, it may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and time of a request) for security and troubleshooting.

How long we keep it

  • Trip searches and locations: not stored after the request is answered.
  • Notification token, settings and watched trips: until you turn notifications off in the app, remove the watch, or uninstall the app (the token stops working and our server deletes it the next time it tries to send).

Your choices

  • Location: turn it off any time in your phone’s Settings or your browser. The app still works with typed addresses.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app’s Alerts tab, which deletes your data from our server, or in your phone’s Settings.
  • To ask what we hold or to have it deleted, contact us below. Because there are no accounts, include the approximate time you turned notifications on.

Children

Toronto Move is a general-audience transit app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
